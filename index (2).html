To Yogi,

You are my first friend in this life, and also my best friend. And I'm not just saying that casually—I'm saying it straight from my heart. I'm truly thankful for everything you've been through with me, from our childhood until now. I've laughed more with you and your family than with anyone else in this world.

From the day we became friends as kids until this very moment, I've never seen even a little bit of pride or attitude in you. No matter what situation I was in, you never left me alone. You've always been there for me.

I still remember the day our families had a fight about the water flow between our houses. That situation broke something inside me. I couldn't even talk to you for three long years, and I cried more than I can explain. When I finally got the chance to talk to you again, the first thing I noticed was your hand. It was bandaged. I cried even more that day because you were hurt.

When I joined college and started staying in the hostel, you were the one friend I told everyone about. Even now, whenever someone asks me about you, I never just say, “Yogi is my friend.” I always say, “He's my best friend.”

There were so many times when my family was going through difficult situations—fight after fight and one problem after another. But whenever I saw you, everything would disappear for a while. Just being around you was enough to make me feel better.

That's why, after I joined college and we couldn't spend time together like before, I missed you so much. Sometimes, I even cried wishing that we could go back to those days.

Even now, when I'm in the hostel and I see my roommates laughing and talking with their friends on video calls for hours, I sometimes feel that same loneliness. And in those moments, the only person I think about is you.

You never get angry with me. You never made me feel like I had to prove that I was still your friend. You never doubted our friendship, and honestly, I miss that so much.

Ni epavumae kopiduvilla, en friend ah irunthaa va nu — atha na romba miss panuren.

I don't know if it's because of someone's evil eye or simply because life is taking us in different directions. We used to sit together and talk face to face, and now most of our conversations are through calls and texts.

But I've decided something, Yogi. From now on, I'm going to make time to meet you in person again. I want us to sit together, talk for hours, laugh like we used to, and create those memories again.

Since 15.12.2017, through everything, you've stayed by my side. That kind of friendship is rare.

And that friendship is you.

— Ice
